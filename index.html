Strictly enforced (sort of)Dress Code

’80s country club chic meets holiday sparkle

Think The Official Preppy Handbook by way of a holiday cocktail party: crisp, colorful and just a little bit extra. Navy and seafoam? Required. Plaid? It's the holidays, so absolutely. Your guest is welcome (and encouraged) to dress the part too.

  • Popped polo collars & sweaters tied over the shoulders
  • Argyle, madras, gingham & holiday tartan
  • Blazers with crests, cable knits & velvet
  • Pearls, headbands, loafers & boat shoes

The fine printAttendance Policy

This registration page is intended for internal use only by AEF staff, partners, and approved affiliates. The event is not open to the public.

Invitations & Guests

Attendance is by invitation only. Each registered attendee may bring one guest, and attendees are responsible for their guest's conduct at the event. Registrations from individuals outside of authorized internal channels will not be accepted.

Use of Information

Information submitted on this form is used solely to plan and coordinate the Allied Elite Financial Holiday Party (headcount, catering and check-in) and will not be shared outside of authorized internal channels.

Changes

AEF reserves the right to modify, cancel, or restrict attendance at any time without notice. If your plans change, please let the events team know as soon as possible.

Confidentiality Notice

This page and all associated materials are confidential and proprietary. If you are not an authorized participant and have accessed this page in error, please exit immediately and notify the event administrator.
